01About

Passionate about intelligent decision-making and robotics, I'm exploring physical AI through dexterous manipulation. My research focuses on sample-efficient reinforcement learning applied to flow-based policies — including normalizing flows and few-step flow matching — with the aim of enabling real-world reinforcement learning without relying on simulation.

Concretely, I study how reinforcement learning can improve imitation-learning policies: an online RL approach that injects expert demonstrations into the RL replay buffer to accelerate convergence of the value function in sparse-reward tasks. In parallel, I work on humanoid robots for loco-manipulation. My longer-term goal is to fine-tune large Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models on long-horizon industrial assembly tasks.

Beside research, I play football, read, and produce music as a hobby beat-maker.
