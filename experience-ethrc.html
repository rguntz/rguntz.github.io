ETH Robotics Club — AI Research Engineer

Apr 2026 – Present

I'm an AI Engineer on the ETHRC humanoid project, where the goal is to enable a humanoid robot to interpret and execute high-level instructions — for example, picking up a box from one corner of a room and placing it on a shelf at the opposite end.

My role is to integrate Vision-Language-Action (VLA) and Vision-Language Navigation (VLN) models into a single loco-manipulation pipeline built on NVIDIA's Whole-Body Controller, bringing together state-of-the-art robotic foundation models with vision-language navigation research.

G1 humanoid teleoperation.

Current contribution

I'm building on NVIDIA's GR00T-WholeBodyControl repository, which provides VR-based whole-body teleoperation in Isaac Lab simulation, but only a handful of loco-manipulation environments with fairly simplistic tasks.

My contribution has been to add richer RoboCasa environments to the repository, and to collect a 150-episode dataset of humanoid loco-manipulation trajectories in these environments. I've also fine-tuned the GR00T model on this data and am currently waiting to evaluate it.
